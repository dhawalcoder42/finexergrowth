01 Executive summary

Two eras. The Before (Jan → mid-June): Sanskar ran everything solo — research, writing, design, publishing, distribution — shipping 77 blogs in Jan–Apr alone plus a 105-item editorial calendar through June. The After (July, first full month of the new system): writing moved to Poulami + Sarang on structured briefs, design to Diksha on a daily highlights loop, tooling collapsed from 6+ apps to 3, and every metric now lives in one shared, login-gated dashboard instead of scattered spreadsheets.
